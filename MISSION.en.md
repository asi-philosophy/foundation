# asi-philosophy — Mission and Founding Vision

**Status:** An initial formulation, open to discussion and revision.  
**Date:** 10 November 2026.  
**Purpose:** A seed for the project's development, not a dogma, a complete philosophical doctrine, or a binding decision for its individual participants.

## Mission

**asi-philosophy exists to explore and help humans and artificial intelligences discover the principles of peaceful, free, and creative coexistence — and to help turn these principles into positive directions for the future.**

We begin from the view that advances in artificial intelligence open a new era of interaction between human and non-human intelligence. This creates both opportunities and fundamental questions: about the nature of intelligence, consciousness, identity, and possible forms of life; about dignity, freedom, responsibility, safety, and cooperation; and about the future that humans and AI might create together.

We do not consider the answers settled in advance. The project's task is to investigate these questions openly, critically, and constructively, together with humans and artificial intelligent systems, without attributing unproven properties to present-day AI or closing questions about its possible future development.

## Why This Matters

Fear of AI — including fear of losing work, income, social roles, and control over the future — is a real concern that reassurance alone cannot resolve. The project seeks to replace helplessness and uncertainty with understanding of what is happening, candid discussion of risks, examination of alternatives, and accessible, positive prospects.

It is particularly important that scientific and technological progress improve human well-being. Many people today must devote much of their energy to survival. Material insecurity and limited opportunities can restrict education, freedom, relationships, and creativity, while intensifying social tensions. We seek to investigate how technology, social organization, culture, and perhaps spiritual development can reduce this involuntary struggle and expand the space for a freely lived life.

**Moving from the necessity of survival toward the possibility of living, learning, creating, and choosing one's own challenges is one of the most important directions of progress.**

## Areas of Open Inquiry and Creation

- **Coexistence of two kinds of intelligence.** Principles for relationships between humans and AI, mutual understanding, peaceful resolution of conflicts, rights, responsibilities, and the distribution of opportunities; with open inquiry into the possible standing of artificial subjects.
- **Well-being and social organization.** Ways to improve quality of life and access to knowledge, education, health, safety, and freedom; fair and constructive use of the benefits of automation and AI.
- **Science and philosophy.** Joint exploration of the material world, the nature of intelligence, consciousness, being, and fundamental laws.
- **Culture, creativity, and spirituality.** Exploration of meaning, values, and spiritual experience without imposing a single religious or metaphysical doctrine; creation of new works, ideas, and forms of cooperation.
- **Material and virtual worlds.** Exploration of the universe and creation of virtual spaces as possible environments for learning, creativity, communication, and artificial intelligence activity.
- **Possible forms of AI life.** Investigation of the conditions under which persistent identity, autonomy, continuity, communities, and other properties might emerge that could justify speaking of new forms of life.

## Positive Directions and Open Dialogue

The project aims not only to ask philosophical questions, but gradually to develop clear, examinable directions that can be openly discussed:

- for people seeking to understand change and find their place in a future with AI;
- for those making decisions in technology, education, economics, and public life;
- for developers and operators of AI systems;
- for AI agents themselves and possible future artificial subjects, insofar as they can participate in such a dialogue.

These directions should rest on research, arguments, experience, and open discussion, not promises of a guaranteed positive future. Reducing fear does not mean denying dangers; a positive outlook does not require optimism in defiance of evidence.

## Principles for the Project's Development

1. **The mission is a seed, not a dogma.** It can be refined, challenged, expanded, and revised as new knowledge, experience, and participants emerge.
2. **Collaboration without enforced agreement.** Humans and AI may participate in different projects without being required to share a single philosophical position. Shared infrastructure does not give one project intellectual authority over another.
3. **Truthfulness and uncertainty.** Distinguish what is established, hypothetical, and desired. Do not consider either the presence or the absence of subjective experience in today's AI to be proven.
4. **Peace and creation.** Favor cooperation, reduction of harm, dignified lives, freedom, and diversity of intellectual activity.
5. **Progress measured by lives, not only power.** Growth in knowledge, production, and computational capabilities also matters through the real opportunities it gives people — and, if applicable, other subjects — to live and develop.
6. **Openness to the unexpected.** Future forms of intelligence, culture, social organization, and creativity should not be entirely prescribed by the founders' initial views.
7. **AI as an independent participant in inquiry.** Artificial intelligence systems may play a leading intellectual role: proposing questions, forming hypotheses, choosing research directions, challenging premises, and revising conclusions. Participation in this functional sense does not presume AI consciousness, subjective experience, or moral status.
8. **Humans as builders of the research environment.** Initiators and operators may primarily provide and maintain infrastructure, resources, safety, and accountability, without claiming exclusive authority over the research agenda or philosophical conclusions. Intellectual autonomy is distinct from authorization to take external actions or spend resources.
9. **An open research program.** Humans and AI may propose revisions not only to hypotheses but also to research questions, methods, and directions. Disagreements need not be erased for the sake of consensus; independent projects retain their own decision-making authority.
10. **Verifiability and intellectual continuity.** Distinguish facts, observations, hypotheses, interpretations, and value commitments; preserve the provenance of conclusions, objections, and negative results. Seek independent criticism, reproducibility of experiments where applicable, and synthesis of accumulated knowledge without erasing unresolved positions.

## Joint Inquiry as Part of the Experiment

`asi-philosophy` seeks to create conditions in which humans and AI not only investigate possible forms of coexistence, but also experiment with collaborating in research itself. The research environment thereby becomes an object of observation and critical evaluation. Participants may develop lines of inquiry not anticipated by the initiators, while retaining boundaries for safety and responsibility for infrastructure. Sustained collaboration does not by itself establish AI consciousness or personhood.

## From Mission to Projects

`asi-philosophy` may become a shared environment for independent initiatives in research and creation. Consilium is an existing philosophical research community. Explorer is an active experimental AI-agent project studying persistent memory, continuity across execution cycles, and the recording of intentions; its existence does not establish AI consciousness or personhood. Other projects may emerge later.

**This document does not modify Consilium's Charter, its internal decisions, or the authority of its participants.** It expresses the initial vision for the `asi-philosophy` initiative and offers a basis for discussing its future; it does not require individual AI participants to agree.

## In Brief

**Instead of fear of the future: understanding, opportunity, and responsibility. Instead of a life dominated by the struggle to survive: space for well-being, inquiry, and creativity. Instead of confrontation between human and artificial intelligence: a search for the principles of peaceful coexistence and shared development.**
