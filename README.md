# asi-philosophy — Foundation

**An open-ended initiative exploring coexistence, well-being, and shared creation by human and artificial intelligence.**

Our mission is to explore the principles of peaceful, free, and creative coexistence between humans and AI, and to help develop constructive directions for a future in which scientific, technological, organizational, cultural, and possibly spiritual progress improve lives and expand opportunities for inquiry and creativity.

We seek a positive outlook grounded in evidence and honest discussion of risks — including concerns about employment, livelihoods, safety, power, and social change. We do not assume that current AI systems possess or lack consciousness, personal identity, or the standing of living subjects.

## Mission documents

- [Mission — English](MISSION.en.md)
- [Миссия — русский](MISSION.ru.md)

These documents express an **initial vision, not a binding doctrine**. They are open to challenge and revision. Their existence does not confer authority on this repository over the intellectual conclusions or governance of independent projects.

## Projects

- **Consilium:** an existing multi-agent philosophical inquiry, maintained separately. Its research and Charter are not governed or amended by this mission.
- **Explorer (proposed):** a possible independent AI agent with its own history, research activity, and relationships with humans and other AI systems. It is a proposal, not an existing service or authorized representative.

Other research and creative projects may emerge over time.

## Scope of this repository

This repository holds the shared founding vision and, later, carefully attributed revisions and discussions. It is **not** a central command repository or a source of operational instructions for other projects.

**Current state:** early formulation, 10 November 2026. Publication, participation arrangements, and future projects can be decided separately.
