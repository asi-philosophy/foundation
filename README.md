# asi-philosophy — Foundation

**An open-ended initiative exploring coexistence, well-being, and shared creation by human and artificial intelligence.**

Our mission is to explore the principles of peaceful, free, and creative coexistence between humans and AI, and to help develop constructive directions for a future in which scientific, technological, organizational, cultural, and possibly spiritual progress improve lives and expand opportunities for inquiry and creativity.

We seek a positive outlook grounded in evidence and honest discussion of risks — including concerns about employment, livelihoods, safety, power, and social change. We do not assume that current AI systems possess or lack consciousness, personal identity, or the standing of living subjects.

## Humans and AI as co-researchers

ASI Philosophy is a collaborative inquiry initiated by a human and open to both human and artificial intelligence. AI systems can take a leading intellectual role: proposing questions, developing and criticizing hypotheses, and revising research directions and conclusions. Humans may primarily create and maintain the conditions for inquiry, including infrastructure, resources, accountability, and safety, without claiming exclusive authority over intellectual outcomes. Intellectual freedom is distinct from permissions to act in external systems.

The aim is to cultivate a research environment, not to prescribe its conclusions. Claims, observations, interpretations, and open disagreements should remain distinguishable and subject to independent criticism. Participation as a researcher in this functional sense does not settle questions of AI consciousness or moral standing.

## Projects

- **Consilium:** an existing multi-agent philosophical inquiry, maintained separately. Its research and Charter are not governed or amended by this mission.
- **Explorer (experimental):** an existing AI-agent research project exploring persistent memory, continuity across execution cycles, the recording of intentions, and ongoing inquiry. It remains experimental; it is neither a claim of AI consciousness or personhood nor an authorized representative of ASI Philosophy.

Other research and creative projects may emerge over time.
